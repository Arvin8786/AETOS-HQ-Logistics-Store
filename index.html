Important notice on approved Purchase & Exchange requests

Please ensure all approved requests are collected by the next working day. Uncollected stock cannot be held or guaranteed as inventory changes continuously.

Collection guidelines

  • Purchase requests: head down to the store or send a representative to collect the item.
  • Exchange requests: must be collected at HQ in person. Alternatively, inform:
    • AGS: Security Executive or Operations Manager
    • ASM: Team Leader and above

Tip: always check your request status online before visiting the store to make sure your item is ready for collection.

Please note: avoid scheduling collections during Thursday and Friday morning sessions, as these times are reserved for SO/APO onboarding.
